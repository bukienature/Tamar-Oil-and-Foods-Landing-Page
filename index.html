Our story

More Than a Brand — A Mission

Tamar Oil and Foods was born out of conviction — an inspiration we know was God-given. What started as a personal calling has grown into a mission: to fight food adulteration and bring pure, natural, wholesome food back to African tables.

We are not just selling palm oil. We are selling a dream — a future where families across Africa, and eventually the world, can trust what's in their kitchen again. It's a long-term vision, built on integrity — one bottle, one customer, one honest product at a time.

Right now, our focus is on perfecting and establishing our palm oil — pure, unadulterated, and traditionally processed. As Tamar Oil and Foods grows, we're also proud to offer quality catfish, with more honest, natural food products to come.

Why Pure Palm Oil Belongs in Your Kitchen

Rooted in West African food traditions, palm oil brings its own color and character to everyday cooking.

  • ✳Vitamin EPalm oil contains vitamin E compounds, including tocotrienols.
  • ◉Naturally vibrantBeta-carotene contributes to palm oil’s characteristic red-orange color.
  • ❋Made for the everyday kitchenA traditional, versatile cooking oil used across West African cuisine for generations.

The Hidden Danger of Adulterated Palm Oil

Across many markets, palm oil is sometimes mixed with cheaper substitutes, artificial dyes, or industrial additives to cut costs — often without consumers ever knowing. This isn't just a quality issue; it's a trust issue that affects households everywhere.

At Tamar Oil and Foods, this is exactly what we exist to fight. Every bottle we sell is a small stand against adulteration — pure palm oil, nothing hidden, nothing added.
